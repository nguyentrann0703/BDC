BDC – Business Decision Challenge 2025

This project is developed for the Hackathon 2025 – Business Decision Challenge, where participants analyze a real-world business case and deliver data-driven insights to support strategic decision-making.

📌 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to explore business performance data, identify key operational issues, and propose actionable solutions. The analysis focuses on uncovering patterns in sales, customer behavior, and operational efficiency in order to support short-term and long-term business improvements.
